# Risk Assessment

## Purpose

The Risk Assessment screen provides a detailed analysis of community risks identified by the DscienTia AI Intelligence Layer.

The objective is to help organizations proactively identify emerging issues, prioritize interventions, and allocate resources effectively before situations escalate.

This screen transforms raw field reports into actionable risk intelligence.

---

# Assessment Components

## Community Overview

Displays:

* Community name
* Project association
* Population estimate
* Current risk level

Purpose:

Provide immediate context for decision-makers.

---

## Risk Score

Displays:

* Overall risk level
* AI confidence score
* Assessment timestamp

### Risk Levels

#### Low

Minor concerns detected.

No immediate intervention required.

---

#### Medium

Potential issue emerging.

Monitoring recommended.

---

#### High

Significant concerns identified.

Immediate review recommended.

---

#### Critical

Urgent intervention required.

Escalation procedures triggered.

---

# Risk Summary

Provides a concise explanation generated by AI.

Example:

Water-related complaints increased by 25% during the past month, indicating growing resource stress within the community.

---

# Contributing Factors

Displays the evidence used by AI.

Examples:

* Community reports
* Survey responses
* Activity outcomes
* Historical incidents
* Impact metrics

Purpose:

Support explainable AI principles.

Users should understand why a risk was generated.

---

# AI Analysis

## Purpose

Explain detected patterns.

The AI Intelligence Layer evaluates:

* Frequency
* Severity
* Geographic concentration
* Historical similarities
* Trend acceleration

Example:

Reports mentioning water access issues increased from 4 reports to 12 reports within 30 days.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Displays:

* Related reports
* Survey findings
* Community observations
* Impact metrics

Information shown:

* Report title
* Reporter
* Date
* Location

Users may open individual reports for verification.
