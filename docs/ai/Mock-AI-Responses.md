# Mock AI Responses

## Purpose

This document provides realistic examples of AI-generated outputs within the DscienTia platform.

The purpose is to:

* Validate AI user experience
* Demonstrate AI capabilities
* Support UI and UX design
* Guide prompt engineering
* Support IBM watsonx and Granite integration

These examples represent expected outputs during the AI Insight MVP phase.

---

# AI Response Categories

The MVP supports four primary AI outputs:

1. Insight Summary
2. Risk Assessment
3. Action Recommendation
4. Impact Trend Analysis

---

# Scenario 1: Water Access Monitoring

## Input Reports

Community: Sukamaju Village

Reports received during the last 30 days:

* Residents report reduced water supply
* Community well operating below capacity
* Increased water collection times
* Local school reports sanitation concerns

---

## AI Insight Summary

```text
Community Insight Summary

Location:
Sukamaju Village

Observation:
Water-related complaints have increased significantly over the last 30 days.

Key Findings:
- 27% increase in water shortage reports
- Sanitation concerns reported by local schools
- Multiple reports indicate declining well performance

Overall Assessment:
The community is experiencing a moderate water access challenge that may worsen if not addressed.

Confidence Score:
89%
```

---

## Risk Assessment

```text
Risk Assessment

Community:
Sukamaju Village

Risk Category:
Water Security

Risk Level:
Medium

Contributing Factors:
- Repeated complaints
- Infrastructure degradation
- Increasing service interruptions

Potential Impact:
- Reduced household access to clean water
- School sanitation issues
- Increased community dissatisfaction

Confidence Score:
84%
```

---

## Recommendation

```text
Recommended Action

Priority:
High

Recommendation:
Conduct a field assessment of the community well within the next 14 days.

Supporting Actions:
- Inspect water infrastructure
- Coordinate temporary water distribution
- Engage local community leaders

Expected Outcome:
Reduce service disruptions and improve water availability.
```
