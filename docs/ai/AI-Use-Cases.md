# AI Use Cases

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence creates value within the DscienTia platform.

The goal is to transform community reports, project activities, and impact metrics into actionable intelligence that supports social impact decision-making.

* AI Architecture

# AI Vision

DscienTia aims to transform social impact management from:

Reactive Reporting

to

Proactive Community Intelligence

Instead of only collecting data, the platform identifies risks, generates insights, recommends actions, and supports evidence-based decision making.

# Core AI Objectives

The AI Intelligence Layer should help organizations:

* Understand community conditions faster
* Detect emerging risks earlier
* Improve intervention planning

# AI Capability Areas

## 1. Insight Generation

### Purpose

Generate meaningful summaries from field reports and project activities.

### Inputs

* Reports
* Activities
* Impact Metrics
* Community Updates

### Outputs

* Executive summaries
* Trend observations
* Community intelligence

### Example

Water-related complaints increased by 18% compared to the previous month.

---

## 2. Risk Detection

### Purpose

Identify emerging issues before they become critical.

### Inputs

* Historical reports
* Incident frequency
* Community indicators
* Geographic patterns

### Outputs

* Risk score
* Risk category
* Confidence score

### Example

Risk Level: High

Confidence: 89%

Reason:

Repeated reports of water shortages from three neighboring communities.

---

## 3. Recommendation Generation

### Purpose

Suggest actions based on identified risks and community conditions.

### Inputs

* Risk Assessments
* Historical interventions
* Community context

### Outputs

* Recommended actions
* Priority level
* Suggested timeline

### Example

Recommendation:

Conduct emergency water distribution within 14 days.

Priority:

High

---

## 4. Impact Trend Analysis

### Purpose

Analyze long-term changes in social impact indicators.

### Inputs

* Impact Metrics
* Project Outcomes
* Beneficiary Data

### Outputs

* Trend direction
* Impact forecasts
* Performance summaries

### Example

Volunteer participation increased by 12% over the previous quarter.

---

## 5. Decision Support Intelligence

### Purpose

Assist managers in prioritizing interventions and allocating resources.

### Inputs

* Risks
* Recommendations
* Project Data
* Community Conditions

### Outputs

* Prioritized actions
* Resource allocation guidance
* Intervention planning support

### Example

Community A requires intervention before Community B due to higher risk exposure.

* Maintain transparency
